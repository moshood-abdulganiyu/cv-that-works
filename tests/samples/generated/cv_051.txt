Summary
A passionate second year student of Agricultural Economics at the University of Nigeria Nsukka who wants to learn to lead student groups and help farmers earn more from what they grow.

I am a 200 level student and my courses so far include Principles of Economics, Farm Management, Introduction to Agricultural Economics, Statistics and Crop Production. I am a hardworking student who likes to ask questions in class and I am dedicated to understanding how the prices of farm products are set in the market.
Last holiday I spent some weeks at my aunt's provision store and farm stall in Enugu where I learned to weigh goods, record sales and count change. I am a fast learner when it comes to numbers and I am a team player when we work in groups for assignments.
I organised the class reading group during the first semester and I try to mentor two classmates who find Statistics difficult. I also enjoy reading about farm prices in the newspaper and discussing them with my father who sells yams.
