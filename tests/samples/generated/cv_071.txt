I am a 400 level Physiotherapy student at Bayero University Kano and I am a hardworking student who likes to help people move without pain. So far I have studied Anatomy, Physiology, Biomechanics, Exercise Therapy and Electrotherapy, and I have done many practical classes in massage, exercises for stroke patients and the use of the ultrasound machine.
I am a dedicated person and I like to learn from the physiotherapists who teach us in the clinic. In the clinical posting at the teaching hospital I watched how patients with back pain, stroke and broken bones were treated, and I helped to set up the treatment couches and clean the equipment. I am also a fast learner when it comes to new exercises.
In the long vacation I spent some weeks at a local sports club in Kano where I watched trainers strap ankles and treat muscle pulls. I learned the names of the common sports injuries and I kept a notebook of exercises that I practise at night.
I belong to the physiotherapy students association and I take part in the weekly meetings. I like to explain simple exercises to my family members when they complain of neck pain and I hope to work in a sports clinic one day.
