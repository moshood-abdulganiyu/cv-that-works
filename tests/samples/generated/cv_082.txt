Summary
A passionate environmental science student at Federal University Lokoja who loves nature and wants an internship where waste can be sorted, trees can be planted and water can be tested for everyone in the community.

Skills
I am skilled in tree planting, waste sorting, water testing with simple kits and the use of Microsoft Word. I am a hardworking and dedicated person and I am a fast learner when a new method is shown to me, and I have planted a total of 400 tree seedlings with my environmental club around Lokoja.
When we do cleanups in the hostels and along the river, I am a team player who carries bags, labels bins and reminds others to wear gloves. I also like to draw posters that tell people not to burn refuse and I have used them in the faculty week.
I can use a pH meter and a turbidity tube under supervision, and I keep a notebook where I write the readings of each test so that I can compare the water from different wells. I hope that one day I can help my town to manage its waste in a cleaner and cheaper way.
