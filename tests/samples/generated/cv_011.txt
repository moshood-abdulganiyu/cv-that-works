I am a 300 level student and my courses so far include Food Chemistry, Food Microbiology, Biochemistry, Food Processing and Unit Operations and Principles of Nutrition. I am a hardworking student who likes to understand the reason behind each step in a practical.
In the laboratory classes I have used the autoclave, the hot air oven, the pH meter and the moisture analyser, and I write my practical reports carefully. I am a fast learner when a new piece of equipment is introduced in class.
I attend the departmental seminars on food safety and I read the NAFDAC guidelines on packaged food labelling so that I understand what the law expects from food producers in Nigeria.
My favourite courses are Food Microbiology and Food Processing because they show how bread, juice, yoghurt and other everyday foods are made and kept safe for people to eat.
