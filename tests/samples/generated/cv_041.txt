Olamide Fashina
Nigeria

I am a 500 level pharmacy student at the University of Ibadan and I am looking for an internship in a hospital or community pharmacy where I can use my knowledge of dispensing, drug information and patient counselling. In my first four years I have studied Pharmaceutical Chemistry, Pharmacology, Pharmaceutics, Pharmacognosy and Clinical Pharmacy, and I have done many practical classes in compounding and quality control of tablets and syrups.
During the long vacation I spent some weeks in a community pharmacy where I learned to arrange drugs on shelves, read prescriptions, count tablets and explain the dose to patients. I also worked with a team of students on a survey of how families store medicines at home in the Bodija area, and we wrote a short report for the department.
I take part in the weekly drug information talks of the Pharmacy Students Association and I like to explain medicine labels in simple words so that patients understand when to take their drugs and when to see a doctor.
