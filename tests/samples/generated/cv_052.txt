I am a 300 level Microbiology student at the University of Port Harcourt and I am a hardworking student who enjoys looking at small living things under the microscope. So far I have studied General Microbiology, Bacteriology, Mycology, Virology and Biochemistry, and in the practical classes we cultured bacteria from soil, water and food.
I am a dedicated person and a fast learner, and I like to read about how diseases spread from one person to another. During the last long vacation I spent three weeks at a private medical lab in Port Harcourt where I watched how blood and urine samples are tested and I washed and sterilised glassware.
I am also a self-motivated person who revises alone in the library every evening. I joined the departmental students association and I once represented my class in a quiz. I like drawing diagrams of cells and I help my friends revise before tests, and I hope to become a medical microbiologist one day.
On weekends I read about food hygiene and I try to explain to my family why food should be covered and stored well. I want to learn more about the microbes that spoil food and the ones that make yoghurt and bread rise.
