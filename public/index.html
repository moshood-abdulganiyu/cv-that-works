How your CV is scored out of 100

  • 20

    Evidence and numbers

    Most bullets carry a number, a scale or a result.

  • 20

    Bullet quality

    Action verb, scope and result. No “helped with” or “responsible for”.

  • 15

    Sections present

    Education, projects, leadership or volunteering, skills and more, in a sensible order.

  • 10

    Opening summary

    Two to three lines: who you are, your goal and one proof point.

  • 10

    Contact and first impression

    Professional email, custom LinkedIn link, phone and city.

  • 10

    Language and voice

    No typos, steady tense and no clichés.

  • 10

    Format and length

    One to two pages, bullets not paragraphs, consistent dates.

  • 5

    Fit to goal

    Matches the target you pick. Skipped if you choose “Not sure”.
